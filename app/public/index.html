THE PIPELINE

Three moves, from raw bytes to reconciled truth.

→
INGEST

Stream compressed batches straight to the pipeline. We inflate and parse them server-side, with no pre-processing on your end.

→
RECONCILE

Match, dedupe, and validate incoming rows against your canonical datasets before anything is committed.

→
SYNC

Fan out the reconciled result to every downstream system in near-real-time, with full lineage.

QUICKSTART

Post a compressed batch.

Orbital accepts Content-Encoding: br and gzip transparently. Compress your batch, POST it to the import endpoint, and poll the job.

Try it in the console →
# compress & upload a batch brotli -q 5 batch.json -o batch.json.br curl -X POST https://api.orbital.example/api/v1/import \ -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \ -H 'Content-Encoding: br' \ --data-binary @batch.json.br # => 202 { "jobId": "job_1041", "status": "queued" }
